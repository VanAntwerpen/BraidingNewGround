Directions

The conference will be held in Logic House on the campus of Maynooth University.

You can get to Maynooth using public transport from Dublin airport. There are different approaches, outlined below.

  • Expressway bus 22: this bus goes a few times per day and should be booked beforehand, average price is 15 euros.

  • Aircoach + Train: take the aircoach, which should be booked beforehand, to Drumcondra railway station and a train to Maynooth, average price is 7+2.6=9.6 euros.

  • Bus 16 or 41 + Train: take either bus 16 or 41 at Dublin airport to Drumcondra railway station and a train to Maynooth, average price is 5.2 euros.
