About the workshop

This workshop will be held at Logic House, Maynooth University in the small beautiful town of Maynooth, Ireland. It aims to bring together the diverse fields of vertex algebras, tensor categories, Hopf-Galois theory and algebraic tools related to the Yang-Baxter Equation to discuss ideas, recent advances and explore potential collaboration.

This workshop can be viewed as a celebration of the recent appointment of Sven Möller and Arne Van Antwerpen at Maynooth University. Hence, the topics of this workshop cover topics close to their research interests, where tensor categories form a meeting point.

There will be a session of lightning talks to which participants can contribute.
