About the workshop

This workshop aims to bring together the diverse fields of vertex algebras, tensor categories, Hopf-Galois theory and algebraic tools related to the Yang-Baxter Equation to discuss ideas, recent advances and explore potential collaboration. Its conception can be viewed as a joint celebration for the recent appointment of both organizers at Maynooth University.

The covered topics may seem rather diverse, but have a meeting point in (braided) tensor categories.

Topics covered

  • Vertex algebras
  • Tensor categories
  • Solutions of the Yang-Baxter Equation
  • Hopf-Galois structures
